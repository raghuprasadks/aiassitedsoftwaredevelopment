20 Full-Stack Project Ideas

Technology Stack:
Programming Language: Python
Backend Framework: Flask
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Database: SQLite

1. Student Study Planner
Manage subjects, assignments, deadlines, study schedules and academic progress.

2. Online Examination System
Admin creates questions, students attend timed examinations, automatic evaluation, score calculation and performance reports.

3. Library Management System
Manage books, student records, book availability, issuing, returns and overdue fines.

4. Mini Learning Management System (LMS)
Teachers upload study materials, create courses and assignments. Students enroll, access materials and track their learning progress.

5.College Event Management System
Create events, manage registrations, track participants and generate event passes.

6. Personal Expense Tracker
Record income and expenses, categorize spending, set monthly budgets and visualize expenses through charts.

7. Team Task Management System
Create projects, assign tasks to team members, set deadlines and monitor task completion.

8. Appointment Booking System
Users can book appointments, check available time slots, reschedule and cancel bookings. Admin manages appointments.

9. Inventory Management System
Manage products, stock levels, suppliers, purchases and sales. Generate low-stock alerts and maintain inventory records.

10. Habit Tracker Application
Create daily habits, track completion, maintain streaks, set goals and visualize progress over time.

11. Campus Lost and Found Portal
Students report lost or found belongings, upload photographs, search for items and contact the respective users.

12. Volunteer Management Platform
Organizations post volunteering opportunities. Students register, participate and track their volunteering hours.

13. Student Discussion Forum
Students create discussion threads, ask questions, comment, like posts and interact with other students.

14. College Canteen Food Ordering System
Display food menus, add items to cart, place orders and track order status. Admin manages food items and orders.

15. Job and Internship Portal
Students create profiles, upload resumes, browse job opportunities, apply for internships and track application status. Companies can post vacancies.

16. Online Customer Support System
Customers submit queries, raise support tickets and track their status. Support staff can respond, assign and resolve tickets.

17. Online Resume Builder
Users enter their education, skills, experience and projects. Generate, preview and download professional resumes.

18. Complaint Management System
Users submit complaints, upload supporting images and track resolution status. Admin assigns and manages complaints.

19. Coding Practice Platform
Students browse programming questions, submit solutions, view scores, maintain submission history and track their progress.

20. Smart Parking Management System
Display available parking slots, book parking spaces, manage vehicle details and track parking bookings.
